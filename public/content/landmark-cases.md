# Landmark and Significant Victories

Case studies demonstrating our track record of success in complex IP litigation.

---

## INEOS v. Sinopec (2010)
**Category:** Trade Secret  
**Client:** INEOS

One of the largest trade secret cases in Chinese history. The settlement resulted in US $7 billion dollars of business for our client over the last decade—one of the most successful settlements in China.

---

## Michael Jordan v. Qiaodan Sports (2016)
**Category:** Trademark  
**Client:** Michael Jordan

Secured 77 victories across numerous cases filed across China with a retrial petition to the Supreme People's Court. Selected as a Supreme Court Model IP case on International IP Day, April 26, 2016. Reported by CCTV.

## Apple v. Qualcomm (2019)
**Category:** Patent (SEP/FRAND)  
**Client:** Apple Inc.

The largest SEP case in China at the time involving standard essential patent licensing disputes. Damages claimed exceed RMB 1.6 billion across more than 30 patent infringement cases before 11 IP Courts and the Supreme People's Court.

---

## Boehringer Ingelheim v. HEC Pharma (2023)
**Category:** Patent  
**Client:** Boehringer Ingelheim

The first and only National Major Patent Infringement Case in China. The judgment was successfully enforced against the defendant across 28 provinces in just 18 months following the initial filing.
